Subdomain Takeover - Proof of Concept

This page is a non-malicious proof of concept hosted as part of a coordinated vulnerability disclosure / bug bounty submission.

It exists solely to demonstrate that a dangling DNS record allows an unauthorized party to serve content on this hostname. No data is collected, no downloads are offered, and no impersonation of the legitimate domain owner is intended.

  • Report ID: [#3680283]
  • Date: [4/17/2026]

The legitimate owner may remove this PoC at any time by reclaiming or deleting the dangling DNS record.
